🔍 Leverage is a magnifier

It magnifies profits and it magnifies losses. High leverage doesn't make a strategy better, it makes mistakes bigger.

Size your positions so that one bad trade can't hurt your account badly.
---
📅 Big news days

CPI, NFP, FOMC and rate decisions can move gold fast, with wider spreads and slippage. Many traders reduce size or stay out around these events.

Check the economic calendar before your trading day starts.
---
🧱 Support & resistance

Price remembers areas where it reversed before. Think in zones, not exact lines, then wait for confirmation before acting.

⏳ Patience pays

📐 Market structure 101

Higher highs + higher lows = uptrend.
Lower highs + lower lows = downtrend.

A break of structure can be an early sign the direction is changing. Wait for confirmation.

🎯 Where do stops cluster?

Often just above recent highs and below recent lows. Price sometimes sweeps those areas before moving the other way.

Keep this in mind when you place your stop-loss.

😤 Avoid revenge trading

After a loss the urge to "win it back" is strong. That's when the worst decisions happen. Step away, review, come back with a plan.
---
🕳️ Weekend gaps

Gold can open on Monday away from Friday's close. Stop-losses may fill at a worse price in a gap. Be careful holding positions over the weekend.

💵 The 1% idea

Many traders risk about 1% of their account per trade. On a $1,000 account that's about $10. The goal is to survive a string of losses, not to hit a jackpot.

🪙 What does XAUUSD mean?

Gold priced in US dollars per troy ounce. On most brokers a standard 1.00 lot is typically 100 ounces, so a $1 move in gold is about $100 per lot. Know your contract size before you trade.
---
📊 Gold and the dollar

Gold is priced in dollars, so a stronger dollar often puts pressure on gold, and a weaker dollar often supports it. It's a tendency, not a rule. Always read the chart too.
---
🚫 More trades is not better

Overtrading eats your capital through spreads and mistakes. Quality setups, fewer trades, clear risk.
